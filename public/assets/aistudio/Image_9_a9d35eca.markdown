## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an editorial, intellectually stimulating, and reflective ethos tailored for founders and pragmatic dreamers. Rooted in Saras Sarasvathy’s Effectuation framework, the interface shuns tech-bro hyper-gloss and cold hyper-minimalism. Instead, it mirrors a masterclass journal or an executive thinking retreat: deliberate, literary, tactile, and grounded in human agency.

### Visual Voice & Personality
- **Reflective yet Action-Oriented:** The UI acts as a calm, seasoned venture counselor rather than an urgent task tracker.
- **Warm Editorial:** Evoking heavy-stock art books, tactile notebook paper, and deliberate typography. Whitespace is generous, dignified, and intentional.
- **Intellectual Clarity:** Complex entrepreneurial principles (Bird-in-Hand, Affordable Loss, Crazy Quilt, Lemonade, Pilot-in-the-Plane) are surfaced through precise typographic hierarchy, disciplined borders, and tactile indicator chips.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Surfaces reject pure optical white in favor of soft alabaster, linen, and bone, eliminating digital fatigue.
- **Disciplined Precision:** Thin, warm hairline rules and restrained borders replace heavy drop shadows. Contrast is achieved via deliberate typographic scale rather than sensory overload.
- **Catalytic Accents:** Interactive elements use terracotta and persimmon to signal action, while sage and muted amber celebrate validation and synthesis.

## Colors

The color palette shifts the digital experience toward fine-press bookmaking and thoughtful executive advisory.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#D96B43` Terracotta / Catalyst):** Reserved for forward momentum, provocative Socratic coaching questions, primary decisions, and active state transitions.
- **Secondary (`#4A6B5D` Deep Sage / Commitments & Validation):** Signifies agreed partnerships, verified resources, stakes placed within affordable loss limits, and confirmed action points.
- **Tertiary (`#D4A373` Muted Golden Amber / Epiphany & Synthesis):** Illuminates distilled insights, core principle tags, and AI-synthesized patterns from founder dialogue.
- **Neutral (`#2D2D2D` Deep Obsidian & Charcoal):** Provides high-clarity structural lines, headings, and high-readability body copy.

### Canvas & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF8F5`):** Warm alabaster backdrop providing an organic ground.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#F4EFEB`):** Soft stone tint for card containers, reflection zones, and conversational bubble backgrounds.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#EFE8E1`):** Active or nested blocks, input wells, and segmented controls.
- **Hairline Strokes (`#E5DCD2`):** Low-contrast structural rules separating thought streams and workspace panes without visual noise.

## Typography

The typographic strategy juxtaposes the classic authority of an editorial serif with the pristine, modern utility of a contemporary geometric sans.

### Type Pairing Architecture
- **Headings & Thought Invocations (`Playfair Display`):** Sets an intellectual, literary tone. Used for Socratic coaching queries, phase headers, and prominent conceptual distillations. It slows the reader down slightly, inspiring deep consideration.
- **Interface & Dialogue Engine (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Chosen for its crisp, geometric balance and exceptional legibility across dense conversational flows, inventory matrices (Means: Who I am, What I know, Whom I know), and tactical commitment cards.
- **Editorial Sub-role (`quote-editorial`):** Styled with an italic variation of Playfair Display, this role delivers poignant AI coaching reframes and pivotal quotes from effectuation literature.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an open reading room—airy, proportioned, and uncrowded.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Responsive Modular Column System:** Desktop utilizes a 12-column structure constrained to a maximum content width of 1280px to protect line lengths and maintain reading rhythm. Tablet transitions to 8 columns, and mobile collapses into a single-column sequence with sticky contextual anchors.
- **Rhythmic Vertical Proportions:** Vertical whitespace adheres strictly to an 8-point base scale, utilizing generous breathing room (`space-lg` and `space-xl`) between conversational threads, coaching provocations, and decision canvases.
- **Split-Pane Coaching Canvas:** On desktop screens, the interface balances conversational coaching on the left (5 columns) with a dynamic Effectuation workspace (Means, Losses, Quilt) on the right (7 columns), separated by a continuous hairline border (`#E5DCD2`).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids high-blur, heavy drop shadows in favor of a flat, layered, paper-like tactile depth.

### Depth Mechanics
- **Tonal Layering:** Surfaces do not hover; they sit upon one another like sheets of fine stationery. Lower tiers take the warm base `#FAF8F5`, while elevated containers shift to `#F4EFEB`.
- **Low-Contrast Ghost Outlines:** Cards, toolbars, and panels utilize a 1px solid border (`#E5DCD2` or `#DBD1C5`). This gives structural discipline without harsh visual borders.
- **Ambient Focus Elevation:** When modal interaction or card dragging is required (e.g., rearranging means or partners in the "Crazy Quilt"), elevation is communicated via an ultra-subtle tinted wash: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(45, 45, 45, 0.06)`, keeping the atmosphere warm and weightless.

## Shapes

The spatial language favors restrained, architectural geometry over overly bubbly or toy-like rounding.

### Corner Curvature Rules
- **Base Components (Cards, Inputs, Dialogue Bubbles):** Standardized on `0.25rem` (Soft, 4px) for a crisp, publication-grade feel.
- **Structural Containers & Modals (`rounded-lg`):** Styled at `0.5rem` (8px) to establish balanced boundaries without softening into casual softness.
- **Effectuation Badges & Principle Pills:** Fully rounded / capsule form factors strictly reserved for metadata tags, principle badges (e.g., "Bird-in-Hand"), and micro-status indicators. This distinct contrast instantly separates conceptual metadata from content containers.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Catalyst Button:** Solid `#D96B43` background with white text, crisp 4px corners, and `0.75rem 1.5rem` padding. Subtle hover state drops opacity to 0.9 with a hairline downward shift (+1px).
- **Secondary / Commitment Button:** Ghost style with a 1px border of `#4A6B5D`, text in `#4A6B5D`, and a transparent canvas. On hover, fills with a 10% tint of sage (`#E5EFE9`).
- **Text / Editorial Action:** Understated serif or sans links with an intentional 1px underline positioned 4px below text baseline, shifting to `#D96B43` on focus.

### Badges & Principle Pills
- Specialized, capsule-shaped tags indicating the 5 Effectuation Principles:
  - **Bird-in-Hand (Means):** Light terracotta wash (`#FAF0EB`) with `#D96B43` typography.
  - **Affordable Loss:** Neutral stone wash (`#EFE8E1`) with `#2D2D2D` typography.
  - **Crazy Quilt (Partnerships):** Soft sage wash (`#E5EFE9`) with `#4A6B5D` typography.
  - **Lemonade (Leveraging Surprises):** Soft amber wash (`#F8F2EB`) with `#B07C4A` typography.
  - **Pilot-in-the-Plane (Agency):** Warm charcoal wash (`#ECE8E3`) with `#1A1A1A` typography.

### Input Fields & Dialogue Composer
- Input fields display an unadorned, bookish aesthetic: a light bone background (`#F4EFEB`), a 1px perimeter of `#E5DCD2`, and an inset padding of `0.75rem 1rem`.
- Focus is designated not by harsh blue rings, but by a 1px stroke shift to `#D96B43` accompanied by a faint warm aura (`0 0 0 1px #D96B43`).

### Cards & Thought Containers
- Built on `#F4EFEB` with a delicate `#E5DCD2` border and `space-md` inner padding.
- Card headers pair an editorial serif title with a right-aligned principle pill badge. Section dividers within cards use 1px dashed or dotted rules in `#E5DCD2`.

### Coaching Dialogue Bubbles
- **AI Coach:** Borderless `#F4EFEB` background with generous typography, incorporating rich editorial formatting, highlighted quotes, and bold catalytic questions.
- **Founder Response:** Subtle outline container (`1px solid #DBD1C5`) on `#FAF8F5`, anchoring the founder's articulated means, commitments, and dilemmas into the shared canvas.
